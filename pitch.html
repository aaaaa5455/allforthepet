The Problem

Dog owners face 3 recurring pain points

01
Fragmented Health Records
Vaccination cards, vet prescriptions, and lab results are scattered across physical documents and multiple apps with no unified view.
02
Nutrition Guesswork
Most owners have no way to track whether their dog is getting the right calories, protein, and nutrients — leading to obesity and deficiencies.
03
No Accessible Expert Advice
Getting a quick answer to "Is this food safe for my dog?" or "Should I see a vet now?" currently means an expensive clinic visit or unreliable online searches.
Our Solution

One platform. Every health need covered.

🤖
AI Health Assistant
24/7 conversational AI answers health questions, flags symptoms, and recommends whether to see a vet — reducing unnecessary clinic visits by up to 40%.
📊
Nutrition & Trend Tracker
Log meals and track calories, protein, and key nutrients. Visual trend charts reveal health patterns over weeks and months before they become problems.
📍
Vet Finder & Records
GPS-powered vet clinic finder shows real nearby clinics with hours and contact info. All medical records — vaccinations, prescriptions, visits — stored in one place.
